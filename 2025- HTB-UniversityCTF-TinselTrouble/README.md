# HackTheBox University CTF 2025: Tinsel Trouble

- **Date:** 19th - 21st December 2025
- **Format:** Jeopardy-style, Team CTF
- **Team:** SJB Institute of Technology
- **Final Rank:** 221st out of 1014 teams (Top 22%)
- **Performance:** Personally got 18 out of 34 flags.

---

### Personal Reflection

Participating in the HackTheBox University CTF was an intense experience. The "Tinsel Trouble" edition brought a highly competitive field of over 1,000 university teams. We secured a spot in the top 22%, but the real victory was our solve rate—we cleared nearly the entire board (29/34 challenges). This event tested my endurance and attention to detail, particularly in the Forensics category, where I spent the majority of my time ensuring we secured every single point available.

### Highlight: The VSM Image Forensics Challenge

My favorite challenge of the event was a complex Forensics investigation involving a VSM image file. Unlike standard challenges that often focus on a single artifact, this was a multi-layered puzzle that required a holistic approach.

-   **The Task:** I had to perform a deep-dive analysis on the image to uncover hidden flags.
-   **The Solve:** The breakthrough came from correlating different data sources. I had to analyze **network traffic** patterns and cross-reference them with **Windows Registry key analysis** to reconstruct the attacker's activity. It was a grueling but satisfying process of digital archaeology that required piecing together fragmented evidence to form a complete picture.

### My Contribution & Key Skills

I served as the primary Forensics specialist for the team, while also supporting the Reversing and Crypto categories.

-   **Forensics (100% Completion):** I successfully solved **all 16 challenges** in the Forensics category. This required a wide range of skills, from memory forensics and packet analysis (Wireshark) to file carving and log analysis. Clearing this entire category was my biggest contribution to our team's score.
-   **Reverse Engineering & Crypto:** I stepped out of my main zone to solve one Reverse Engineering challenge and one Crypto challenge. The reversing task involved static analysis to understand the binary's control flow, while the crypto challenge required identifying and breaking a specific encoding scheme.

### Key Takeaways

-   **Domination in Forensics:** Clearing 16/16 forensics challenges validated my workflow and methodology. It taught me the importance of having a robust toolkit for registry and network analysis ready to go.
-   **Cross-Domain Correlation:** The VSM challenge reinforced that forensics is rarely about looking at one file; it’s about connecting network events to system changes (registry keys).
-   **Team Velocity:** Ranking 221st in such a large pool showed that while accuracy is key, speed is the next frontier we need to work on to break into the top 10%.
