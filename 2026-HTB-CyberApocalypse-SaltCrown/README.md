### Overview

Cyber Apocalypse is HackTheBox's biggest open CTF of the year, and "The Salt Crown" was the largest field I've competed in so far, with 6,744 teams. The event ran for six days. We finished inside the top 5% and solved over three quarters of the board.

### Key Takeaways

-   **Scale:** This was the biggest event in this logbook by number of teams, and it is our best percentile finish in a large open team CTF.
-   **Endurance:** A six-day CTF with 136 challenges rewards steady output over the full week as much as fast early solves.
-   **Next step:** We got no first bloods. Solving faster in the first hours after release is the clearest area to improve.
